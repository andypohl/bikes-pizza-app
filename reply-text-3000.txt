Hello, and thank you for reviewing bikes.pizza. A screen recording from an iPhone on the current iOS is attached.

1. Screen recording: it starts at launch and shows browsing, account creation with email verification, commenting, submitting a photo, the store, and account deletion. Other features like direct messages can be shown in another video if needed.

2. Purpose and audience: bikes.pizza is a small community for people who like bicycles and pizza. Members share photos of bikes and pizzas with a short story, and everyone can browse, react, comment, and message each other, with strong moderation so the feeds stay on topic. The audience is adults and teens 13 and over. The app is free, with no ads, in-app purchases, analytics or tracking; the only commerce is a small merchandise store handled by Shopify.

3. Main features:
- Browsing, search and the store need no account. The All, Pizza and Bikes tabs list posts; tap one to open it.
- Sign in from the Settings tab with the demo account under Sign-in required (email and password, no second factor). New accounts can be created with email, Google or Apple; email accounts must verify the address before posting.
- With an account: react and comment under a post; tap a member's username → Message to start a conversation; the envelope icon opens conversations.
- Reporting and blocking: every post has a flag button; every comment and conversation has Report in its menu; a conversation's menu also has Block; Settings → Report a concern takes any report, including "Child safety concern". Reports go to our moderators; a comment reported by more than one member is hidden until reviewed.
- Submitting a photo: Bikes or Pizza tab → "Submit a bike" / "Submit a pizza". Submissions are screened automatically (photos showing people are refused), then reviewed by a person before publication, so nothing submitted during review will appear publicly. Any library photo works.
- Account deletion: Settings → Delete account → confirm, or https://bikes.pizza/delete-account.
- Store: the Store tab lists our merchandise; buying opens Shopify checkout.

4. External services: Firebase (authentication, database, storage, functions, hosting); Google Cloud Vision (photo screening); Google Cloud Natural Language (comment and message screening); Mailgun (email); Shopify (store and checkout); Apple and Google sign-in. No advertising, analytics, tracking or AI-generation services.

5. Regional differences: none. English only; the store ships where Shopify's checkout allows.

6. Regulated industry or protected material: not applicable. All photos and text are contributed by members under our Terms of Use; the merchandise is our own brand. Policies: https://bikes.pizza/terms, https://bikes.pizza/privacy, https://bikes.pizza/child-safety.
